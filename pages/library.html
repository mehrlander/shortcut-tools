Shortcuts

The library organized on axes the Shortcuts app does not have. Nothing on this page deletes anything.

Four steps, and deletion is only the last one. The page nominates, you stage into a holding folder, the folder sits there as long as you like, and you delete on the device with the shortcut open in front of you. A stage is a move, so every step before the last is reversible.
